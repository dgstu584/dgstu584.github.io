FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the Arsenal Shop in-game and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.

How do I remove ads?
Purchase the Commander Pack from the Arsenal Shop to permanently remove all ads.

I found a bug — what do I do?
Email us at deusgamestudio@gmail.com with a description of the issue and your device model.
