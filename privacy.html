PRIVACY POLICY

DEUS GAME STUDIO

Last updated: April 28, 2026

INTRODUCTION

Deus Game Studio ("we", "our", "us") develops and publishes mobile games including Nuke Down and Bug Ring Siege. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our apps.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Our games do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information. We do not directly collect, store, or process any personal data.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our apps use the following third-party services, which may collect certain data as described in their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob — We display ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Before any tracking occurs, our apps display Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, allowing you to choose whether to allow tracking. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple Game Center — If you use Game Center features such as leaderboards, Apple handles that data according to Apple's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat — We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases. RevenueCat may process transaction data as described in RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Our games store gameplay data such as high scores, settings, and purchase status locally on your device using standard browser storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our games are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
